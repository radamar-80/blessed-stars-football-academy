Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

1. Who we are

This website is blessedstarsfa.com, operated by Blessed Stars Football Academy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you use the site or register a player, how we use it and how we protect it.

2. Information we collect

We only collect the information you choose to send through the registration form on the Contact page. The form fields are:

  • Guardian name
  • Player name
  • Player age
  • Age group
  • Phone number
  • Any message you write

Because the form opens a WhatsApp chat, the message is sent to our WhatsApp account and stored by WhatsApp under their own privacy policy. We do not store the form data on our own server.

3. Information about minors

Our players are minors. We only accept registration information from a parent or legal guardian. The registration form requires a consent checkbox to confirm this. We do not collect information directly from children.

4. How we use the information

We use the information you send only to:

  • Reply to your enquiry
  • Arrange a trial session or registration
  • Contact you about the squad your player is joining

We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties for marketing.

5. Cookies and analytics

This site does not use cookies, analytics services or tracking pixels. We do not use advertising or remarketing tools.

6. Third party services

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts. When your browser requests these fonts, Google receives your IP address. Google's privacy policy is published at policies.google.com/privacy.

WhatsApp is used to deliver registration messages. WhatsApp's privacy policy is published at whatsapp.com/legal.

7. Photos and videos of players

From time to time the academy takes photos and videos during training and matches. We only publish images of players when we have written consent from their parent or guardian. Consent can be withdrawn at any time by contacting us.

8. Security

Registration messages are delivered through WhatsApp, which uses end to end encryption. Access to our WhatsApp account is restricted to academy coaches and staff who need it to respond to enquiries.

9. Your rights

You can ask us to delete any message or record we hold that relates to you or your child. You can also withdraw photo and video consent at any time. To make a request, contact us at [EMAIL ADDRESS].

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, email [EMAIL ADDRESS].
